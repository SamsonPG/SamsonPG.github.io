How I work

Gates, not good intentions

CI fails on architecture violations, missing permission guards, undocumented modules, and public claims without evidence. Rules a person has to remember are rules that decay.

Prove the check can fail

Every gate I add, I break on purpose first and watch it go red. A check that cannot fail is worse than none, because it is believed.

Say the uncomfortable part

TryTokka's threat model states plainly what an attacker with the deployment key could do. Publishing the limits is what makes the rest credible.
