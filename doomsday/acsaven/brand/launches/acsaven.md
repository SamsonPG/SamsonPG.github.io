## Dev.to (studio intro article — title + body)

**Title**
```
Acsaven: building private Try tools that stay with you
```

**Tags:** `webdev`, `opensource`, `privacy`, `productivity`, `showdev`

**Body**
```
I’m Samson PG. I run Acsaven — a small studio behind the Try family.

## The idea

Most “free tools” ask you to upload a photo, paste a JWT, or type your grades into someone else’s server. We build the opposite: tools that do the job in your browser, or (for spend) tell you when an AI invoice is about to surprise you.

## The products

1. **TryQuickImg** — HEIC, compress to a KB target, resize, crop. Processing stays on-device.  
   https://tryquickimg.com/

2. **TryDevSnip** — JSON format/diff, cron, timestamps, JWT decode — without handing secrets to a stranger’s server.  
   https://trydevsnip.com/

3. **TryCalculatingNow** — final grade, GPA, SIP/EMI, GST, invoices — no signup wall for basic jobs.  
   https://trycalculatingnow.com/

4. **TryTokka** — synced AI API spend across providers; Scout emails when you cross your limit after daily sync. Not TikTok.  
   https://trytokka.com/

## Studio home

https://acsaven.com/

If you try any of them, tell me what broke or what you wished existed next. That’s how we ship.
```
