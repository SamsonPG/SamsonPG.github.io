## TryQuickImg

```
Hey Peerlist 👋 I’m Samson (Acsaven).

TryQuickImg is a free image toolkit that runs in your browser — HEIC→JPG, compress to a KB target, resize, crop, QR, and light PDF helpers. Files aren’t uploaded to our servers for processing.

Built it because “upload your passport/exam photo to convert” felt wrong. Would love feedback from anyone who’s shipped local-first tools.

## TryDevSnip

```
Hey Peerlist 👋 I’m Samson (Acsaven).
