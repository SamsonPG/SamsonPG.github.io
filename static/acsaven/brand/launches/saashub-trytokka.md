```
Invoice-oriented AI API spend across providers in one place, with Scout emails when you cross the limit you set after daily sync — not a chat meter, not a proxy, and not TikTok.
```

**2. Why should a person choose TryTokka over its competitors?**
```
Usage is split across OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, and friends until the card statement shows a spike. TryTokka syncs spend, shows spike cause, and emails Scout alerts after sync — with a free trial and clear Solo/Team pricing.
```

**3. How would you describe the primary audience of TryTokka?**
```
Indie builders and small teams shipping with AI APIs who want invoice-level spend visibility and threshold emails — not social video users.
```

**4. What's the story behind TryTokka?**

```
Surprise AI bills kept showing up across multiple provider dashboards. TryTokka is an Acsaven product: Scout watches API invoices so surprise bills don’t sneak up on you.
```

```
Next.js, Supabase, Stripe/Razorpay/Dodo, Resend for Scout mail, AES-256 key encryption — SaaS spend dashboard for AI APIs.
```
