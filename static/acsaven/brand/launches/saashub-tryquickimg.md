## 8) Q&A

**1. Unique?**
```
Processing stays in your browser — photos are not uploaded to our servers for conversion, compression, resize, or crop. You can hit exact KB targets for forms and portals, including HEIC from iPhone, with no signup and no watermark.
```

**2. Why us?**
```
Most free converters ask you to upload first. TryQuickImg skips that for core tools: HEIC→JPG, compress-to-KB, resize, crop, QR, and light PDF helpers run locally after the page loads. Built for exam photos, passport pics, and email size limits.
```

**3. Audience?**
```
Students, job applicants, and anyone who needs portal-ready or email-ready images without uploading private photos to a random converter.
```

**4. Story?**
```
We kept hitting “upload your photo to convert” flows for everyday jobs like KB limits and HEIC. TryQuickImg is an Acsaven product built so those jobs stay on your device.
```

**5. Tech?**
```
Astro, Tailwind, and client-side libraries for HEIC, image compression, PDF, and QR — deployed on Cloudflare. Tool work runs in the browser.
```

**6. Customers?**
```
- Individual students and applicants using free browser tools
- Independent users converting HEIC or hitting form KB limits
- Acsaven studio users across the Try family
```
