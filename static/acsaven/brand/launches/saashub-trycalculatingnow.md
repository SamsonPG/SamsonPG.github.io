## 8) Q&A tab (all 6)

**1. What makes TryCalculatingNow unique?**
```
Honest grade and money math without a signup wall for basic jobs. Calculators run in your browser so grades and numbers aren’t trapped behind an account first.
```

**2. Why should a person choose TryCalculatingNow over its competitors?**
```
Many calculator sites force signup or feel cluttered. TryCalculatingNow focuses on finals, GPA, SIP/EMI, GST, invoices, and everyday % — free for everyday jobs, with privacy-first browser processing.
```

**3. How would you describe the primary audience of TryCalculatingNow?**
```
Students checking finals and GPA, plus anyone who needs practical finance or percent math without creating an account first.
```

**4. What's the story behind TryCalculatingNow?**
```
Signup walls on simple grade math felt silly. TryCalculatingNow is an Acsaven product built so “what do I need on my final?” stays fast, free, and local.
```

**5. Which are the primary technologies used for building TryCalculatingNow?**
```
Astro, Tailwind, and client-side math — deployed on Cloudflare. Inputs are not uploaded to our servers for processing.
```

**6. Who are some of the biggest customers of TryCalculatingNow?**
```
- Students using free finals and GPA calculators
```
